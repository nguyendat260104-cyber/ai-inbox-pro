## 1. 3 Short-Form Video Concepts

- **Channel:** TikTok / YouTube Shorts

- **Core Concept:** Show a chaotic desktop screen with 20 open browser tabs (Trello, Google Sheets, Slack, sticky notes), then transition to a clean, all-in-one ClickUp 3.0 workspace where tasks, docs, and chat are consolidated.

- **Channel:** YouTube Shorts / LinkedIn Video
