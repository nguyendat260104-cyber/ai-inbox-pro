## 2. Priority Offers In-Depth Teardown

### #1. Make.com (Celonis)
- **Official Source:** [https://www.make.com/en/affiliate-program](https://www.make.com/en/affiliate-program) (Verified 2026)
- **Commercial Terms:** **35% recurring commission** on all subscription plan payments for **12 months** from the user's registration date. *(Excludes additional operation pack add-ons).*
- **Attribution & Cookie:** 90-day cookie window. Attribution relies on unique first-party affiliate link.
- **Approval Process:** Instant to 48-hour approval. Minimal application friction; requires specifying promotional channels (YouTube, TikTok, website).
- **Vietnam Payout Practicality:** **Superior**. Make uses **Wise** exclusively for commission distribution. Wise allows instant currency conversion and withdrawal directly into any Vietnamese domestic bank account (Techcombank, Vietcombank, ACB, MB Bank) in VND with near-zero wire friction. Minimum payout threshold is **$100**, and the partner must have referred at least 3 unique paying customers to initiate automated payout batches.
- **Prohibited Methods:** Bidding on trademark terms (`Make`, `Make.com`, `Integromat`), unauthorized coupon claims, and self-referrals are strictly banned.
- **Target Personas:**
  1. *Agency Operations Leads:* Fatigued by manual copy-pasting of Facebook Lead Ads to CRM and Telegram.
  2. *Automation / No-Code Freelancers:* Looking for cost-effective alternatives to Zapier's steep pricing tiers.
- **Buyer-Intent Keywords:** `make com tutorial tieng viet`, `thay the zapier gia re`, `tu dong hoa gui lead telegram tu facebook ads`.
