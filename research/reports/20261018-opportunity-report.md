## 4. Market Dynamics & Vietnam Compatibility

1. **Payment Methods**:
   Vietnamese buyers strongly favor tools that accept domestic debit/credit cards or local digital wallets (MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay). Hostinger and Canva excel here due to localized payment gateways. For B2B/SaaS tools (Make, Notion, ElevenLabs), Visa/Mastercard adoption among tech professionals is over 80%.
2. **Pricing Tolerance**:
   Micro-SaaS pricing between \$5–\$25/month sees rapid consumer adoption. Higher tier products (\$80+/mo like Semrush or Surfer SEO) must be pitched specifically to agencies, marketing leads, or revenue-generating freelance studios.
3. **Traffic Channels**:
   TikTok and YouTube Shorts drive the highest discovery volume, while YouTube long-form tutorials and Google Search SEO yield the highest conversion intent.
