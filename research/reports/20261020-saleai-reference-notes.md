# SaleAI reference notes for AI Inbox Pro

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-20 (+07:00)  
**Source:** https://sale.ai.vn/ (official product site)

## What is directly visible on the public page

- The hero pairs a short sales-assistant promise with three concrete outcomes: cross-channel replies, remembered customer context, and follow-up/reporting. It then offers two clear actions and shows product imagery.
- A trust strip follows the hero. The site publishes a claim of 2,000+ businesses and displays partner marks; these are SaleAI's own public claims, not independently verified here.
- The product story continues through a selectable audience/use-case area, a mobile app/inbox demonstration, industry examples, pricing tiers, capability explanations, integrations, and FAQ.
- The inbox mock makes channel filters and human handoff states legible. Public page text names states such as “AI đang chat”, “Chờ nhân viên”, and “Nhân viên hỗ trợ đang xử lý”.
- The page provides a pricing route, a simulation CTA, and a visible user journey from initial assistant value to operational details and FAQ.

## What we can infer (not facts about internal implementation)

- The product narrative is stronger when it stays close to recognizable seller jobs instead of leading with broad AI terminology.
- The long page earns its length by changing evidence type between sections: product image, audience-specific scenario, operating view, pricing, integrations, and FAQ.
- Human/AI collaboration feels more believable when the mock labels the actual handoff state rather than relying on a generic “AI is working” status.

## Adaptation for AI Inbox Pro

- Keep the user's existing distinct product position: one inbox for commerce, grounded replies from approved catalog/context, and a human-controlled handoff. Do not imitate SaleAI's copy or pricing.
- Make the first screen lead with one clear product promise, then show a legible conversation → grounded suggestion → human action sequence as the proof.
- Replace the generic process columns with a purposeful sequence and concrete state labels. Keep all mock data visibly illustrative.
- Add audience/use-case content only when the local product actually supports the stated workflow. Do not invent customer logos, adoption metrics, conversion claims, awards, or integration status.
- Structure the remainder of the Landing by evidence and user questions: how it works, what operators control, supported/available channels (clearly distinguished from configured status), and a concise FAQ/CTA.
